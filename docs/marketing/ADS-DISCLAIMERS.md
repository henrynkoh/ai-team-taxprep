# Advertising and post disclaimers (tax / education)

Use **one** short disclaimer on paid social and near CTAs. Adjust for platform character limits.

## English — short (social)

`Educational cohort for tax professionals. Not personalized tax advice. Outcomes vary. Verify with IRS publications, software, and your own professional judgment.`

## English — medium (blog footer)

`This content is for educational purposes only. Tax laws change; software updates change. Nothing here is tax, legal, or investment advice. Consult a qualified tax professional and verify all positions against official IRS guidance and your tax software before filing or advising clients.`

## Korean — short (네이버·티스토리)

`세금·회계 교육 목적입니다. 개별 상담·세무자문이 아닙니다. 세법·해설·프로그램은 변경될 수 있으니 공식 자료와 전문가 확인이 필요합니다.`

## Paid ads (Meta / Google-style)

- Do **not** claim guaranteed savings, audit-proof results, or “IRS loophole” without qualification.
- Prefer: **professional development**, **workflow training**, **TurboTax Desktop + LLM process**, **Seattle-area niche**.
- Target: **tax professionals** or **students of tax** where allowed; follow each platform’s financial services and professional certification policies.

## Lead magnet / signup

Include: “By requesting materials, you confirm you understand this is educational content and not a client engagement.”
