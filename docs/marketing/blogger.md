## Sample post (Markdown → paste into Blogger HTML or compose)

### Intro

Greater Seattle sits at an unusual intersection: **high W-2 tech income with RSU vesting**, **short-term rental** activity, and **adult family home (AFH)** rentals — sometimes on the **same return**, **single filing status**. Software can handle the math only when the preparer gets depreciation, reporting character, and basis right.

This article describes an **educational** 7-day curriculum for **tax professionals** who want a **TurboTax Desktop-centered workflow** and a **prompt library** using **Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini** for mechanical support — always followed by **human verification** against IRS publications and current software behavior.

**Disclaimer:** This is not individualized tax, legal, or investment advice. Laws and software change. Verify all positions with official guidance and a qualified professional.

---

### What the cohort covers (summary)

- **Day 1:** Sub-niche positioning + full TurboTax Desktop screen map for the income combo.  
- **Day 2:** Media flywheel + LLM-assisted content (Reels ideas, LinkedIn carousels, local hooks).  
- **Day 3:** Full manual sample return + timestamped documentation.  
- **Day 4:** “Agent-style” prompt chains + orchestration diagram.  
- **Day 5:** Onboarding scripts, proof dashboards, pricing language.  
- **Day 6:** Multi-year and compounding workflows + case study outlines.  
- **Day 7:** 90-day plan + consolidated playbook.

*(Standard vs advanced tracks differ in depth and hours per day.)*

---

### Why three LLMs?

Different models **hallucinate** or **omit** different details. Running the **same prompt** in **Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini** teaches triage: which model is better for research-style prompts vs calculation scaffolding vs TurboTax “click-path” language — with **you** as the final reviewer.
